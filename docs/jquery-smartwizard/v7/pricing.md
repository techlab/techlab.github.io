## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can I use the Community version in commercial projects?

Yes! jQuery SmartWizard Community is licensed under the **GNU General Public License v3.0 (GPLv3)**, which permits commercial use. However, if you distribute applications containing jQuery SmartWizard, you must comply with the GPLv3 licensing requirements, including applicable source-code and redistribution obligations. If you are building closed-source commercial applications and prefer not to comply with GPLv3 distribution requirements, you can purchase a Commercial License.

### Why should I purchase a Commercial License?

A Commercial License allows you to use jQuery SmartWizard in proprietary and closed-source commercial applications without the distribution obligations of GPLv3. It also provides access to commercial support, depending on your selected plan, and helps fund ongoing development, maintenance, and new features.

### Is the Commercial License a one-time payment or subscription?

All jQuery SmartWizard v7 commercial licenses are **one-time purchases** with no recurring subscription fees. Each license includes lifetime updates within the jQuery SmartWizard v7.x release series. Major future versions, such as v8, may require a separate license or upgrade purchase.

### Can I use jQuery Smart Wizard in my SaaS application?

Yes! You can use jQuery SmartWizard as part of your commercial SaaS application under the appropriate Commercial License. However, redistributing jQuery SmartWizard as a standalone product, embedding it into another product for resale, or offering it as a component for others to build upon requires additional OEM or redistribution rights.

### Do I get lifetime updates?

Yes! All commercial licenses include lifetime updates for the jQuery SmartWizard v7.x series, including bug fixes, improvements, and new features released within that major version. Major version upgrades may require a separate purchase.

### How do I receive support?

Community users can report issues and ask questions through [GitHub Discussions](https://github.com/techlab/jquery-smartwizard/discussions). Commercial license holders receive direct email support according to their selected plan.
